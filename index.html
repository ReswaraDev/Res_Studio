Starting My Journey in Game Development

ReswaraDev is my personal digital space and development log as an indie game developer in training. It all started out of pure curiosity back in Minecraft, where I discovered Command Blocks and began experimenting with building simple mini-games inside them. From that fascination with game logic within pixelated blocks, my curiosity grew into a passion to learn game development from scratch. This portfolio is purely built to document every step of my learning process, experiments, and small milestones along this creative journey.
